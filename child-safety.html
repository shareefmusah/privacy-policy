Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention

Published by Shareef Technologies · Effective Date: November 15, 2026 · Target: Gist Campus Platform
Zero Tolerance Policy: Gist strictly and categorically prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation, or child sexual endangerment. Any violation results in immediate, permanent account termination and automatic escalation to legal and regulatory authorities.

1. Standards & Prohibited Conduct

Gist is a campus network designed exclusively for tertiary university students. Our safety guidelines maintain an unequivocal zero-tolerance stance towards child exploitation. The following acts are strictly banned and considered serious criminal matters:

  • Creation, uploading, transmission, sharing, viewing, or storing of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, or grooming of minors.
  • Solicitation, predation, harassment, or inappropriate messaging directed toward individuals under the age of 18.
  • Depicting or encouraging sexual violence or harm against children.

2. In-App Reporting & Detection Mechanisms

Gist provides multiple transparent in-app mechanisms that empower students and moderators to instantly report suspicious, harmful, or abusive activity:

  • In-App Content Reporting: Every post, reel, comment, and chat message features a dedicated Report button. Users can select "Child Safety / Inappropriate Content" to trigger priority administrative review.
  • User Profile Reporting & Blocking: Students can report and block any profile suspected of violating child safety or platform community standards.
  • Automated & Priority Escalation: Content reported under child safety triggers an immediate freeze of the post or media item and routes the incident to our emergency safety queue.

3. Cooperation with Authorities & NCMEC

Gist complies fully with all applicable international, regional, and national child protection laws:

  • Mandatory Reporting: Whenever suspected Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or exploitation is detected, Gist immediately preserves digital evidence and submits a formal report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional law enforcement.
  • Law Enforcement Assistance: We cooperate actively with the Cyber Security Authority (CSA) Ghana, the Ghana Police Service, and international child protection taskforces in investigations concerning child exploitation.
  • Legal Preservation: Records related to child safety incidents are securely stored and quarantined in compliance with legal hold guidelines to support ongoing criminal prosecutions.

4. Enforcement & Remediation Actions

Upon receipt or detection of a child safety violation:

  1. The offending user account is permanently terminated and hardware-banned immediately.
  2. All offending content is eradicated across our storage backends and content distribution networks.
  3. Digital forensics and forensic logs are quarantined for legal disclosure.
  4. Formal criminal referral is completed with authorities.
